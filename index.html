Jarvis Life OS

Personal productivity assistant

Jarvis Life OS is a personal, non-commercial productivity application. It uses the Google Drive API to read, organize and create files in the Google Drive account of the person who authorizes it, only after explicit consent through Google OAuth.

The app is not offered to the public, is not sold, and does not display advertising.

Jarvis Life OS é um aplicativo pessoal de produtividade, sem fins comerciais. Usa a API do Google Drive para ler, organizar e criar arquivos no Google Drive de quem o autoriza, apenas após consentimento explícito via OAuth do Google.
